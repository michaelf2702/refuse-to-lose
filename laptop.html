        What this demo is built on

        Confirmed in the handover: Brick Tracker columns Date, Member ID, Spanish, Online Skill/Business, Training, Content, Reading, Budget, Bricks, Notes; Bricks = =COUNTIF(C2:H2,TRUE); member totals = =SUMIFS('Brick Tracker'!$I:$I,'Brick Tracker'!$B:$B,C$1); the wall never resets; fund targets £45,000 and £30,000.

        Demo rules, for the founders to approve: a streak counts consecutive days with at least one brick, and today does not break it until the day ends; momentum compares the last 7 days with the 7 before (High, Steady or Building); Legacy Score = lifetime bricks × 10 + current streak × 5; a new level every 100 bricks; team streak counts days when all three builders laid a brick.

        Business HQ: a deal at Deposit paid creates a build for Jack and sends 20% of the setup fee to the Freedom Fund. A build marked Live adds the client to the Client Hub and their monthly fee to the totals. Logging a lead, booking, deal or outreach lays a Business brick for whoever is selected in Building as.

        Everything starts from zero. Enter your fund starting balances on the Funds tab. Data saves in this browser only until shared data is built.
